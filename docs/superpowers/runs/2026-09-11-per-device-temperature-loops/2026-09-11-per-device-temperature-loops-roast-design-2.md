## Rejected (with reason)
- §2.4 Uncontrollable parks both thermal candidates unconditionally with no temperature ceiling/dwell/exit-on-hot (suggested Blocking) — unanimous REJECT: all three seats ran the finding's own spike on both field runs and found 0 of ~26 000 ambient/charger-argmax samples with max(cpu_temp, gpu_temp) ≥ 75 °C (max 73.75 °C); since the argmax is taken over the same sensors as the groups, a hot group becomes the argmax and exits the state; the shadow's err<0 instant fall and the die/Tctl guards run regardless of TStarSource state. The finding's own kill criterion was met.
- §2.5 "Auto entry is deferred while `draw` is `None`" makes Auto un-enterable with the dGPU unpowered (suggested Should-fix) — unanimous REJECT: §2.3 step 1's `group_c is None → GroupUnavailable, cap = max` branch is evaluated first and needs no `draw`; eb9.7/eb9.14 already specify and test exactly this leg ("no GPU lock written, CPU loop regulates alone"); the sentence describes per-loop seeding, not a `SetAuto` gate. Wording could forward-reference the group_c branch.
- §2.4 T* ceiling `cpu_hot_c − 2` is a Tctl threshold applied to an EC-group quantity, admitting Tctl ≈ 94 °C (suggested Should-fix) — reproduce and refute REJECT: the independent CPU hot guard (§2.5 step 3) compares real Tctl to `cpu_hot_c = 90` in matching units every sample and ratchets the cap before the T* ceiling could let Tctl approach 95; at the observed worst offset (+5.75 °C) the guard trips at group ≈ 84 °C, before the 88 °C ceiling binds. The units mismatch is a naming nit; ground's CONFIRM stands as a minority view.
- §2.3 step 8 GroupUnavailable resolves toward *more* power (cap = max) with only the latched trips remaining (suggested Should-fix) — unanimous REJECT on a false premise: the CPU (Tctl/k10temp) and GPU (NVML die) hot guards read sensors independent of the EC group that went None and keep ratcheting `max` at 90/88 °C, so a released device inherits the ratcheted ceiling; the "stock restore raises the fast limit" chain belongs to the whole-EC Released path, not this one. The asymmetry with DrawUnavailable/ActuatorMismatch has no stated rationale — worth one line when the dwell entry above is fixed.
- §4 sim 10's no-relay rule cannot be evaluated (1.5 cycles at 20 min in a 30-min window) and the ±50 % perturbation is 6 vs 26 runs (suggested Should-fix) — reproduce and refute REJECT: the bar is a peak-to-peak/reversal check (a 20-min period shows a full peak and trough inside 30 min, distinguishable from drift); eb9.12's gate names sims 1–4 and 5–8, not sim 10; "each perturbed by ±50 %" is a routine sensitivity-run instruction. A one-line note on run count would not hurt.
- §2.3 write cadence gates the Mismatch re-write behind "the quantised cap changes", deadlocking the freeze (suggested Should-fix) — unanimous REJECT: §2.5 step 5 states "the Mismatch re-write… and the reassert paths are unchanged", i.e. the carried mechanism reasserts every reassert period regardless of cap change (controller.rs:2018-2023 compares against the last *verified* value with a comment to that effect); exposition could name it in §2.3's exception list.
